## Study and adaptation

The source-linked library contains the previous 20 agencies and 20 creator accounts. It labels official services, agency case studies, creator websites and editorial profiles separately. The strongest detailed evidence informs five playbooks:

- TSL and family outing publishers: useful local discovery guides, companion short video and a clear destination. GoBela adapts this as a cinematic weekend story and a saveable guide.
- Kobe's Geniebook case and Partipost's experience-based campaign examples: specific audience matching and experience-led content. GoBela adapts the method, without borrowing endorsements, reach claims or creative assets.
- Our Little Playnest: useful resources followed by a relevant next step. GoBela proposes a practical shared-activity guide, without clinical or developmental outcome promises.
- HEPMIL, GOODSTUPH and Annette Lee's documented parenting humour: recognisable local problems and emotional payoff. GoBela proposes planning overload transformed into family time.
- Heroes of Digital, Narrators and Brew's described measurement approach: connect the journey to meaningful outcomes. GoBela uses only its explicit campaign/enquiry/booking linkage. Ad clicks, saves and views remain unconnected.

These are proposed adaptations, not independently proven superior tactics. Creator editorial profiles do not establish current audience geography or campaign results. The TSLA and MoMo Twins entries explicitly disclose limited methodological evidence. No media, likeness, endorsement or usage rights were imported.

## Prepared work

Three local database campaign drafts are saved, each with source URLs, a caption, hook, storyboard, audience, objective, measurement proposal and release blockers:

1. One less weekend to plan — recommended first with no performance evidence.
2. What a family activity actually feels like.
3. A small family moment worth making time for.

Stable campaign UUIDs and insert-on-conflict-ignore preserve founder edits and prevent duplicate preparation, including concurrent requests. A later founder read returns the saved record. Preparation creates no channel action and makes no approval or execution transition.

Ranking defaults to editorial fit. If one of these strategy-linked campaigns has recorded completed bookings, it ranks above those with fewer linked completions; the UI still discloses that counts are observational and are not causal or spend-adjusted comparisons. The proposed 14-day review is a recommendation, not an active schedule.

Only active catalogue records with genuine HTTPS sources, valid non-negative prices, and review dates within the last 30 days are selected. Future, stale, malformed and example-domain records are excluded. No suitable listings are currently present in the local fixture database, so release is held pending catalogue verification. No artificial verification timestamps were added.

The approved family cast, original logo, cinematic backpack benchmark, native resolution and narrative QA requirements are embedded in the briefs. Finished media has not been generated. No private health/family tables are read by the marketing endpoint.
