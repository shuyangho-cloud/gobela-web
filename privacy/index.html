Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 May 2026

GoBela ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by GoBela Pte. Ltd., a company registered in Singapore. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the GoBela mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

Account details (name, email address, password), child profile information (first name, age range), and community posts and reviews you submit.

Information collected automatically:

Location data (to show nearby activities — only when the app is in use), app usage and screen views (via Google Analytics), crash reports and error logs (via Sentry), and device type and operating system.

Purchase information:

Subscription and purchase history (processed by Apple and RevenueCat — we do not store your payment card details).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to personalise activity and meal recommendations for your family, show events and classes near your location, process subscriptions and in-app purchases, improve app stability and fix bugs, and send optional notifications about new activities (you can opt out at any time).

3. Third-Party Services

GoBela uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

4. Children's Privacy

GoBela is designed for parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Child profile information (name, age) entered by parents is used solely to personalise recommendations and is never shared with third parties.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the third-party service providers listed above, and only as necessary to operate the app.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

Under the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and applicable law, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct inaccurate data, withdraw consent and request deletion of your data, and opt out of marketing communications. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@gobela.sg.

8. Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data. Account passwords are never stored in plain text.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. Continued use of GoBela after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
